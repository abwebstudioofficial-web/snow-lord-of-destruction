The story

Snow rules the district from a white mansion beyond the pass. His right hand, the Snow General, holds a stone keep above the district. Between you and both of them: a horde that never stops coming, and streets you will learn roof by roof.

Fight through the district, climb the General’s stairs, claim a blade forged in the first winter — and find out what Snow really did to the people who used to live here.

What’s in it

A full campaign, start to credits, with checkpoints so you never lose more than a stage.

Six weapons

Pistol, SMG, M416, bolt rifle, shotgun — and the Frost Blade, with Frost Nova and Glacier Dash.

Loot the rooftops

Locked chests up on the roofs hold laser sights and a red-dot scope — if you can pick the locks. Armor and grenades are out there too.

Ash

A cat who rides on your arm — until you need him to be the size of a boss.

Two bosses

The Snow General on his keep, and Snow in his throne hall. Both change the fight at half health.

Voiced cutscenes

A fully voiced story, told between the fights — and some of it you only learn at the very end.

Your way

Third person, over the shoulder, or first person. Adjustable FOV, rebindable keys, stealth if you want it.

Install

About a minute. Nothing else to download.

  1. Download

    Snow-The-Lord-of-Destruction-win64.zip (116 MB).

  2. Extract

    Right-click the zip → Extract All… → Extract.

  3. Install

    Open the folder and double-click Install Snow.cmd. It adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts. Or just run SnowLordOfDestruction.exe to play without installing.

“Windows protected your PC”? That appears for any app that isn’t code-signed, which costs money most indie games don’t spend. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only see it once. To remove the game later: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Snow – The Lord of Destruction → Uninstall.
